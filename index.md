# Isaac Agudo

> Associate Professor of Computer Science at the University of Málaga, researching applied cryptography at NICS Lab, and founder of Decentralized Security.

I teach and research applied cryptography at the University of Málaga, where I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and a member of NICS Lab. I am also the founder of Decentralized Security.

## Research lines

- **Secure multi-party computation**: How current MPC protocols behave in concrete scenarios, with a focus on the trade-offs between performance and security.
- **Applied cryptography for blockchain and connected vehicles**: Security problems in blockchain and connected-car settings, and new solutions for privacy, authentication and confidentiality.
- **Digital identity**: Requirements for interoperable identity schemes, including self-sovereign identity, blockchain-based solutions and wearable devices as identity enablers.
- **Authentication, authorization and delegation**: Design and implementation of privacy-friendly authentication and access delegation, including proxy re-encryption.
