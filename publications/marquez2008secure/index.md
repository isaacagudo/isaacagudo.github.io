# Secure Real-Time Integration of Services in a OSGi Distributed Environment

- Authors: Jose Manuel Marquez, Javier Jimenez, Isaac Agudo
- Type: Conference paper
- Venue: Conference paper in *2008 Fourth International Conference on Networked Computing and Advanced Information Management*, pp. 631–635.
- Year: 2008
- DOI: [10.1109/ncm.2008.173](https://doi.org/10.1109/ncm.2008.173)
- Page: https://isaacagudo.github.io/publications/marquez2008secure/

Abstract: This paper presents a service oriented architecture for real-time integration of services, how to distribute them in a local domain and how to define a secure way of accessing resources using users' and services' authorization and authentication. This work take advantage of previous European R&D projects focused on del.ivering applications and utilities in embedded real-time environments and the convergence of different worlds like Internet and digital TV.
